# Skill icons

Source: [Umamusume Wiki — Game:List of Skills](https://umamusume.fandom.com/wiki/Game:List_of_Skills)

The icons are game assets © Cygames, retrieved from the wiki, which publishes its
content under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The files are named after the wiki's icon id. `web/data/skill_icons.json` maps
`master.mdb` skill ids to those icon ids.
